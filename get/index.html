Sync

Your book on every device, in your own Dropbox

Connect your own Dropbox and the same book opens on your PC, Mac, iPhone and iPad, formatting and all. Between computers, OneDrive, Google Drive and iCloud Drive work too.

  • Your Dropbox, not ours. Unwip has no server. Your files go from your device to your Dropbox and nowhere else.
  • Nothing gets written over. If two devices change the same chapter, both versions are kept and you choose.
  • Works offline. Write anywhere; it catches up when you’re back online.
  • Safe saves. Every save is written in full before it replaces the last, with backups and snapshots behind it.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, while it’s in beta. At launch it will be a one-time purchase, never a subscription. If you tested it during the beta, you keep it free.

Does it use AI?

No. Nothing in Unwip writes, rewrites or suggests for you. Spelling, grammar and readability checks follow fixed rules and run on your device.

Is my writing private?

Yes. Unwip has no account and no server of its own, so your writing never passes through Unwip. It stays on your devices, and in your own cloud if you connect one. Nothing is used to train AI, and there’s no tracking.

Can I bring my book in from Scrivener, Word or Google Docs?

Yes, and from Obsidian and Markdown. You see how it will land before anything is made, and your original isn’t changed. You can send the book back out to any of them, any time.

Is Proof of writing proof I wrote it?

It shows how the words reached the page: what was typed, deleted and pasted, and when. It isn’t foolproof on its own (someone could retype words from elsewhere), so treat it as strong supporting evidence, not a guarantee.

Why does my computer warn me before it opens?

Unwip isn’t registered with Microsoft or Apple yet (that costs a yearly fee). On Windows choose More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac, open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway. You only do it once.

Who owns what I make, and can I sell it?

What you make is yours. Unwip claims no ownership of anything you write or make with it: your books, notes, maps, coats of arms, rune alphabets, sigils, insignia, magic systems, stat systems, character sheets and every file you export. Your work stays on your device, and in your own Dropbox if you connect it. Unwip has no account and never uploads it.

Use it anywhere, including commercially. Use what you make in anything, including work you sell: books, covers, maps, games, art and merchandise. Unwip’s built-in shapes, symbols, fonts and styles can be part of it, changed and remixed however you like. You don’t need to credit Unwip.

What you can’t do. Don’t sell, share or give away Unwip’s built-in artwork on its own, as itself: for example as a symbol or stamp pack, brushes, clip art, stock images or templates made of it with little or no change of your own, and don’t use it to build a competing asset pack or tool. Once it’s part of something you made, that’s up to you.

Other people’s work inside Unwip. Some fonts, symbols, the word games’ dictionary and parts of the software were made by others and are used under their own licences (Open-source licences lists them all with their notices). Those licences come first, and some allow more than this page does. The animal and object symbols from game-icons.net are free to use, even commercially, but ask for a short credit when you publish a picture that uses them, for example on your book’s copyright page: “Symbols by Lorc and Delapouite, game-icons.net, CC BY 3.0”. Build shows the exact line for each picture.

Check your own use. Coats of arms, sigils, runes, insignia and names you make with Unwip, including with its random buttons, can look like real arms, national or official emblems, trademarks or names by coincidence. Before you use one publicly, especially commercially, check it doesn’t copy someone else’s.

How do I report a problem?

In Unwip, click Report a problem at the bottom of the main screen. It adds your version and device (never your writing, file names or book titles) and shows you exactly what’s sent. Or post it in the Discord.
